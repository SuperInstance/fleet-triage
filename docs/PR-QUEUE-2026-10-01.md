### The 2 test failures in `pong-quilt` were my sandbox, not the PR

`conflict-marker-glue.test.js` failed 2 of 7 with `gpg failed to sign the data`. The test
builds a fixture repo and runs `git commit`; this environment forces commit signing and has

```

```

**The failures were the environment's, and I confirmed that rather than assuming it.** A
failure I attributed to the sandbox without checking would have been a wrong merge
decision.

### Ordering mattered, and the ordering was determinable

`fleet-murmur#9` re-pins its corpus to **`pong-quilt` main merge `d51631e`**, and asserts
that corpus is "VERIFIED present-tense against" that commit.

`pong-quilt#87` and `#88` are **Rounds 68 and 69**. `d51631e` is Round 67's head, and it
**is** current `pong-quilt` main.

**So `fleet-murmur#9` had to merge first.** Had the pong-quilt rounds landed ahead of it,
its central factual claim — that the corpus is verified against *main* — would have been
false the moment they merged. Merging in creation order or numeric order would have
introduced a stale pin silently.

## The conflict: `quilt-tools#32` and `#33`

**These must not be auto-merged, and the reason is not a text conflict.**

Both are in `experiments/`, both touch `referral_graph.{pins,seed}.mjs` and
`REFERRAL_GRAPH.md`, and both book **"the fourteenth VERIFIED edge."** They are booking
**different edges**:

- **#32** books `qe-eproc-witness → ds-esign-drift` (edge 14, PENDING→VERIFIED on
  `delta-shape#1`'s merge)
- **#33** books `pq-named-refusals → fm-refusal-ledger` (edge 15, VERIFIED on
  `fleet-murmur#8`)

And they give **mutually inconsistent accounts of fleet-murmur's inbound mass**:

- **#32** says fleet-murmur carries **double** mass (two inbound) and the top view is a tie
- **#33** says fleet-murmur carries **triple** mass (honesty#2 + qgs-adapter#3 +
  refusal-ledger#8) and leads the view outright

**Each is correct against `main` alone. Together they are not.** Measured on the merged
seed:

```
total edges: 19   VERIFIED: 15   PENDING: 4
VERIFIED inbound:  ds-esign-drift 1 <- qe-eproc-witness
                   (fleet-murmur 3 inbound; delta-shape 1 — the first time)
```

So the correct merged state is **19 edges / 15 VERIFIED / 4 PENDING**, with fleet-murmur on
triple mass and delta-shape entering the view. **Both PRs' pins assert fourteen VERIFIED and
eighteen edges** — each individually right, jointly wrong by exactly one edge.

### Why I stopped rather than resolving it

I tried the mechanical resolution first — keep both sides, set the counter to 19. **It
produced a file that does not parse**: the two sides of one conflict were the *same*
template literal with a different number in it, not additive lines, and concatenating them
produced `SyntaxError: missing ) after argument list`.

**"Keep both sides" is not a merge resolution when the two sides are competing values of the
same expression.** I would have been pushing a file I could not run.

The remaining work is not mechanical either. The "fourteenth edge" narrative has to be
rewritten to fifteenth, the view percentages recomputed, and the two accounts of
fleet-murmur's mass reconciled against the merged graph. That is a judgement about the
fleet's own provenance, and it belongs to whoever owns that graph.

**What is needed to finish it:** a branch carrying both seeds, the pin at 19/15/4, both
narratives in the doc, and the view recomputed — then run `node experiments/referral_graph.pins.mjs`
(which passes 113/113 on #33 alone) to confirm.
