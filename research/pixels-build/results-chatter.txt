    total flips    FIXED 9216   FLAT 9216   ratio 1.000
    peak/frame     FIXED 7   FLAT 1024   ratio 146x
    total flips    REDRAW 5698924 (618.37x FIXED)   peak 565
    total flips    TEMPORAL 643192 (70x FIXED)  peak 86
    total flips    PHASED 10766 (1.17x FIXED)   peak 7

    -> FLAT and FIXED agree on the TOTAL to 0.00% and differ 146x on the
       PEAK.  A dither is a re-phasing: it cannot change how many times
       z^10*10 crosses an integer, only how many cells cross together.
       FLAT is the control that scores badly — same information, no spread,
       the whole wall flips at once.  That is the flicker being described.
    -> REDRAW has 81x the peak of the real algorithm and 618.37x its total.
       The retracted churn figure was measuring THIS, which is why it was
       an artifact and why it was such a dramatic one.
    -> TEMPORAL, the cure as literally prescribed, is 70x WORSE than what
       the game already does.  Per-frame dither variation is not
       anti-aliasing; on a quantiser this steep it is extra noise.

C5  THE ALPHABET IS ORTHOGONAL TO ALL OF THIS
    "@&#8x*,:. " and ".:-=+*#%@" are both 10-glyph density ramps.  Swapping one for the
    other changes no flip count in any arm above, because the flip count is
    a property of the quantiser and not of the characters.  The text-priority
    trap in ASCII-VISION-LANDSCAPE.md is real and it is a SEPARATE decision.
